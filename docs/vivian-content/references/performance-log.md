# Performance log - what has actually been tested

Every A/B or posted result goes here. Entries are evidence, not law - n is tiny and confounders are real. When a pattern repeats across 3+ entries, promote it to a hard rule in hooks.md / frameworks.md / voice.md and note the promotion here.

**Read this file before Modes 2, 3, 4 and 5.** Rank hook mechanics and endings by what is in here, not by what sounds good.

Entry format: date, topic, variants, winner, on which metric, the read, the confounders.

## E2 - Networking / "surface area for luck" (essay A/B, both posted, Aug 2026)

> **PARTIALLY CORRECTED BY E6.** This entry claimed "neither converted followers."
> V1 in fact converted 66 followers on 45k views (1.47/1k), which is her baseline
> rate, not zero. The claim was made with no follows data in hand. Findings below
> about reach hold; the follows reasoning was guesswork presented as fact.

**V1 (won on views, by a lot).** Transmissible-fact hook ("you're less than four people away from anyone in the world") → the fact turned on the viewer ("the person who could change your life is a friend of a friend of a friend") → a named borrowed concept, credited to a friend ("your surface area for luck") → she said yes to a cocktail night → honest anti-climax (met nobody, mostly ate) → open commitment (one every fortnight, we'll see).

**V2 (lost).** Complaint hook ("i hate networking and now i think i know why") → the event was bad, left knowing nobody → reframe (networking is just finding people who care what you care about, so share publicly and let them find you) → meta close ("so i guess me posting here is me networking").

**Result: V1 well ahead on views (45k, 66 follows). V2 not separately measured.**

### Why V1 beat V2 on reach

1. **Transmissible fact > first-person complaint, as an essay opener.** "You're four people from anyone alive" is information a stranger can repeat at dinner. "I hate networking" requires them to already care about her. Cold reach comes from lines the viewer can re-transmit. **Scope note: this is a REACH finding only.** V1's conversion (1.47/1k) is baseline. A fact hook buys views at her normal follow rate.
2. **Portable units drive reach.** V1 carried two screenshot-able assets: the four-degrees fact and a *named* idea ("surface area for luck"). V2 carried none. If the central idea has no name, name it.
3. **Open thread > resolved take.** V1 ends unfinished ("we'll see if anything comes of it"). V2 closes fully, and closes by pointing at itself. A finished argument has no episode 2.
4. **Do not argue the viewer out of the action.** V2's conclusion is effectively "don't go, post instead" - it removes the thing the viewer could do, and it happens to justify the behaviour she was already doing. Reframes that conveniently excuse the creator read as self-serving even when they are true. **Confirmed a second time by E5.** Now a hard rule in SKILL.md.
5. **Complaint hooks must be followed by a contradictory action.** "i hate X so i'm doing X" yes, "i hate X so i'm not doing X" no.

### Why V1's conversion was only baseline (rewritten with E6 data)

- **Anecdote is not identity.** One event and a joke about the food is a story about an evening. A stranger finishes V1 knowing a fact about the world and little about Vivian.
- **The smartest line belonged to someone else.** "A friend called it your surface area for luck" is correct to credit, but the most quotable thing in the video is not hers. Credit the source, then out-say it with her own line.
- **A commitment without a counter is not a series.** "One every fortnight" is a quest with no episode numbers. Number it and it becomes F5.

### Confounders

Both posted organically, no paid, different days, single account. Topic overlap means V2 was second exposure to the same idea for part of the audience.

## E3 - 吃苦 vs the embarrassment/tall poppy essay (Aug 2026)

> **RANKING WITHDRAWN, CONCLUSIONS REBUILT BY E6.** This entry ranked the two on
> recollection with no metric attached, and got it backwards on views. Real numbers:
> tall poppy 75k/100 (1.33/1k), 吃苦 20k/30 (1.50/1k). Tall poppy won reach by 3.75x
> and lost conversion. Both readings below have been rewritten against the data.

### 吃苦

Pure F3 Borrowed Concept. Transmissible-fact hook ("there's a word that only exists in Chinese") → literal translation reveal ("eat bitterness") → the concept enumerated → English/Chinese contrast as two flat consecutive statements → the compliment reframe → one line of family ("it's what my parents say about their parents") → aphoristic close ("nothing you swallow is ever wasted").

### Embarrassment / tall poppy

Question hook ("why is it so embarrassing to be seen trying?") → school memory as second-person recognition → tall poppy syndrome named at roughly the midpoint → the jealousy-disguised-as-humility reframe → close that turns four rhetorical questions onto the viewer ("what did you feel when i said that? and if you felt bad, is it because you want the same thing?").

### The read (rewritten)

1. **Recognition openers take reach.** Tall poppy's 75k is the largest reach in the log. "Why is it so embarrassing to be seen trying?" names a feeling almost everyone has and nobody has words for. That is a genuinely powerful reach mechanic and should keep being used.
2. **Recognition without inventory does not convert.** Tall poppy converts worst in the log despite the biggest reach. The close asks the viewer to confess while the narrator confesses nothing. **E3's original finding #5 named this exact mechanism as the cause of a loss that had not happened - the reasoning was right, the metric was wrong.** It is now supported by conversion data and promoted to the Kill Test.
3. **A video with no "I" in it cannot carry an identity payload.** 吃苦 never uses the first person once and converts at baseline (1.50/1k) off small reach. It is a well-made video that tells a stranger nothing about Vivian.
4. **Novelty of the unit drives reach, not conversion.** 吃苦's unit is novel and its reach was the smallest in the log, so the original "novel unit beats better argument" claim is unsupported in both directions. Withdrawn.

### Confounders

Chinese-character content is distributed into diaspora and language-curious audiences that Australia-specific content cannot reach, so the two had different addressable audiences before a word of craft. The embarrassment essay was built from Substack sources (Mode 3), so its emotional engine is second-hand. Different post dates, organic, single account, no paid.

## E4 - The saturday/sunday "retire by 30" DITL (posted, her own draft, Aug 2026)

**Result: ~2k views. Worst result in the log by an order of magnitude. Follows unknown, assume near zero.**

The script: paradox-ish opener ("i want to retire by 30, so on saturdays i work and on sundays i'm not allowed to") → straw man ("everyone acts like you have to be constantly on the grind") → the system explained (lock-in days vs off days) → co-working footage narrated → the rule restated → close on "it made me more productive and i feel more present."

### The read

1. **The reframe let her off completely.** Every consequence was positive: more productive, more present, better protected work time. Nothing admitted, risked or lost. Second occurrence (E2 V2 was the first). **Promoted to a hard rule in SKILL.md.**
2. **Retire-by-30 is saturated for her audience.** At least the third exposure to the same ambition frame. Vivian's own call: "overdone." Dead as a thesis; survives as a subordinate clause for stakes.
3. **DITL as a container is not the problem; DITL as a subject is.** The video narrated the co-working space instead of using it as proof of anything. Mode 1's rule held: footage is evidence, never subject.
4. **Fully resolved ending, no open thread.**

### Confounders

Single post, organic, no paid. 2k is one video and small-account view counts are high-variance. Posting time and day unknown.

## E6 - THE DREAMS RESULT (Aug 2026) - the only clear signal in this log

**40k views, 200 follows, 5.00 follows/1k. 3.75x the next best video. Metric: follows-per-reach, her north-star.**

### The script, verbatim

```
[audio effect] having too many dreams feels exactly like having none.

i've wanted to be a doctor, a chef, a lawyer, an artist, a young mum, own a
company, work in tech, start a podcast, move overseas, own a horse, live on a farm

[speed up / overlap]
every version felt equally exciting, so instead of choosing, i started doing
everything.

i have a beauty brand i want to grow to 7 figures this year but i also just
started another business.

i have a notes app full of half-started ideas, a book i stopped reading on page
40, a domain i bought for a business i never built, and three vibe coded apps i'm
halfway through and yesterday i signed up for horse riding lessons.

[slow down]
it feels like everyday i wake up and have to choose what to let down today

people tell you to just pick a path and commit to it.

but choosing one life means giving up on all the other lives i could've lived.

i keep calling it ambition. but is it just commitment issues?
```

### The autopsy

- **Hook mechanic:** paradox, 9 words, states a feeling as a fact. No "you", no accusation, no information. The viewer supplies the recognition themselves.
- **Beats:** paradox → the wants list (11 items, sped up and overlapping) → the admission of the strategy ("so instead of choosing, i started doing everything") → **her own symptom inventory with forensic specificity** → the cost line, slowed down → the received advice → the price of taking it → self-accusation as an open question.
- **The turn:** at "it feels like everyday i wake up and have to choose what to let down today." The pace drop is the turn; the words alone don't mark it.
- **Ending style:** self-diagnosis phrased as a question, aimed inward, unresolved. **New style, added to frameworks.md as #9.**
- **Follow trigger:** the question is genuinely unanswered and it is about her. A stranger leaves wanting to know whether she picks something. Compare tall poppy, where the question is answered rhetorically and aimed outward, and there is nothing to come back for.
- **Classification: NEW MECHANISM.** Written up as **F9 The Self-Inventory** in frameworks.md.

### Why it beat everything else

1. **Recognition through self-inventory, not through accusation.** The single cleanest finding in this log. Dreams and tall poppy end on the same rhetorical device - an accusing question about ambition - pointed in opposite directions. Turned inward: 5.00/1k. Turned outward: 1.33/1k. The viewer recognises themselves in her list without ever being addressed, and recognising yourself in someone's confession creates an obligation to them that being told about yourself does not.
2. **The specifics do the work.** Page 40. The domain never built. Three half-finished apps. Horse riding booked yesterday. Every one is checkable, mundane and slightly embarrassing. This is the "literal-match the key claims" rule of visual-craft.md applied to language rather than footage, and it is why the list reads as inventory rather than as a bit.
3. **The flex is smuggled in as a symptom.** "A beauty brand i want to grow to 7 figures this year" sits inside a list of problems. The understated-flex rule of voice.md executed better than anywhere else in the corpus: the achievement is load-bearing evidence for the confession, so it cannot read as a flex.
4. **The pacing enacts the argument.** Speed-up and overlap on the wants list makes the viewer *feel* the overwhelm; the slow-down at the cost line makes them feel the weight. The edit is the thesis. Harvested into visual-craft.md.
5. **Identity payload and open thread both present, for the first time in the log.** Stake: a 7-figure target this year, plus a second business. Cost: choosing what to let down daily. Open thread: is it ambition or commitment issues, unanswered.
6. **Zero advice.** It never tells the viewer to do anything. The one piece of advice in the script is quoted in order to price it ("people tell you to just pick a path" → "but choosing one life means giving up on all the other lives"). Advice gets views, identity gets follows - first result that isolates this cleanly.

### Rules promoted from E6

- **F9 The Self-Inventory** added to frameworks.md as the highest-converting known shape.
- **Ending style #9** (self-diagnosis as an open question, aimed inward) added to frameworks.md.
- **Kill Test addition:** "accusation without inventory" - if the video asks the viewer to recognise something in themselves and the narrator's own receipts are not on screen, it converts at roughly a third of the rate.
- **SKILL.md hard rule 5 rewritten.** The old default ("i hate X so i'm doing X") was never supported by a conversion result - it was inferred from E2 V2's failure, which is evidence about the *action half*, not evidence that the shape is her best opener. Demoted to a strong secondary. F9 is the new default.
- **Reach and conversion are tracked separately from here.** Every future entry records both.

### Confounders (real, and they matter)

- **n=1 on this format.** One video. The 3.75x gap is large enough to act on and small enough in sample to be partly luck.
- **Growing account.** A later post converts against a different base and a different audience composition. Post order across the five videos is not recorded and should be.
- **The 200 figure is a total, not attributed.** Some fraction may be spillover from another post or a spike unrelated to this video.
- **Topic advantage is unquantified.** "Too many dreams" is a feeling common to a very large audience of ambitious young women, which is her exact target demographic. The subject may be doing work the mechanism is getting credit for. **The test that separates them: run F9 on a different subject.** If it converts above 3/1k again, it is the mechanism.
- **Nothing here has been A/B tested.** Five posts, five different topics, five different days.
