# Hooks - taxonomy + rules

A hook's only job: open a gap that the video is obligated to close. It must implicate HER (stakes) or the VIEWER (recognition). Hooks that describe the video ("come watch my day in Shanghai") are banned.

## Mechanics (from corpus + adapted to Vivian)

1. **Confession** - self-implicating present-tense problem. "I accidentally started posting in a niche I can't monetize." / "I lied to you guys."
   - Vivian: "I have two businesses and I still can't quit my job." / "I started a pet brand and I don't even know if it'll work."
2. **Paradox (DEFAULT - tested)** - self-contradicting truth in ≤10 words, stated as a fact about a feeling. No second person, no accusation, no information. The viewer supplies the recognition themselves.
   - TESTED: her best converting opener at 5.00 follows/1k, 3.75x baseline. "having too many dreams feels exactly like having none." (performance-log E6)
   - Vivian: "the most disciplined thing i do is nothing." / "i'm busiest on the days i get the least done."
   - Guard: the paradox alone is not what converts. It has to open an F9 self-inventory. A paradox followed by advice converts at baseline.
3. **Contrarian flex** - achievement + immediate subversion. "I moved to London for my dream job at 21. And I hated it."
   - Vivian: "I make [$X] a year at 23. It's not enough and I'll tell you why." / "I had a great childhood. That's my unfair advantage."
   - RETIRED FRAME: "retire by 30" is dead as a thesis (E4, ~2k views, her own call: "overdone"). It survives only as a subordinate clause for stakes.
4. **Universal callout** - name a specific tribe's exact feeling. "If you're the first in your family to move away, you know this feeling."
   - Vivian: "If your parents never bought anything for themselves, you know this feeling." / "If you grew up translating for your parents..."
5. **Borrowed wisdom** - concept from a culture/language/philosophy. "There's a Japanese philosophy that says everyone has three faces."
   - Vivian: "There's a Chinese word that has no English translation, and it explains my entire family."
6. **Delusional quest** - dare-shaped question. "What would happen if you were completely delusional for 100 days?"
   - Vivian: "Can I get my new business its first sale in 24 hours?" / "I gave myself 13 weeks to ___."
7. **Gap-opener** - call out the missing knowledge. "Everyone wants to talk about X but no one actually tells you how."
   - Vivian: "Everyone tells you to start a business. No one tells you which one." 
8. **Second-person read** - "I know you. There are 16 tabs open in your browser right now."
   - Vivian: "You've rewritten that resignation letter in your head about 40 times."
9. **Borrowed quote flipped** - open on an aphorism, make it personal by line 2. "One day you'll realize no one was watching... this quote changed my life."
10. **Audacity claim** - blunt controversial stance, no softening. Her native register.
    - Vivian: "I'm not scared to say I want to be rich." / "Everyone can do what I do. Most people are just lazy." / "Audacity got me further than talent ever did."
11. **Transmissible fact** - a surprising, verifiable fact about the world, turned on the viewer by line 2. TESTED: her strongest reach opener so far (performance-log E2 - "you're less than four people away from anyone in the world").
    - Vivian: "you're less than four people away from anyone alive."
    - **Caveat, tested:** information hooks recruit an information audience. Reach-heavy, follow-light. Only use one when her stake is stated within the next two beats, or the video will win views and no followers.
12. **The stated contradiction** - "i hate X so i'm doing X." Native to her register. The contradiction is stated flat and left unresolved; the video is the evidence, not the explanation. No "here's why", no lesson.
    - **Demoted from DEFAULT (Aug 2026, performance-log E6).** It was never supported by a conversion result - it was inferred from E2 V2's failure, which is evidence about the action half of the sentence, not evidence that the shape is her strongest opener. Mechanic 2 + F9 now holds the default slot on measured data. Mechanic 12 remains a strong secondary.
    - Vivian: "i hate networking so i'm going to an event every week this quarter."
    - Guard: the X must be something she'd actually admit to hating, and the second half must be an action she is taking. Complaint plus avoidance lost a live A/B. Do not resolve it in the ending - the unresolved contradiction is the follow trigger.
13. **Expectation subversion** (OBSERVED, Batch 1 - untested for her) - state the genre, then puncture it in the same breath. "day in the life of a big tech engineer. wait, you thought this was going to be aesthetic?"
    - Vivian: "day in my life running two businesses. no, it's not aesthetic." / "grwm but i'm just going to my 9-5." This is the hook that LICENSES raw, un-cinematic footage - the direct tool for her "show more of my real life" goal.
14. **Revenge / underdog** (OBSERVED, Batch 1) - "to all the people who laughed at me, i [did the thing]." Real identity pull; pair with receipts.
    - Vivian: use with the understated-flex rule intact. "everyone thought starting a business at 20 was a phase. i made my first 100k before i finished uni." Then leave an open thread, never a mic drop.
15. **Self-questioning** (OBSERVED, Batch 1) - an honest internal audit as the opener. "am i actually passionate about this, or do i just like the reward system?"
    - Vivian: "do i actually want to be rich, or do i just want to prove something?" Strong series/essay opener for Pillar 1/3.
16. **Gratitude reframe** (OBSERVED, Batch 1) - reframe the thing people resent as the privilege they once begged for. "what a privilege it is to be tired from the work you once wished for."
    - Vivian: save-y and follow-light (F8) - only worth it with a personal specific attached, or it converts nothing.

**Baseline is ~1.3-1.5 follows/1k.** Three of her four measured videos sit in that band regardless of mechanic, topic or reach. Only one thing has ever broken it. If a hook is not attached to an inventory of her own receipts, assume baseline no matter how good the line is.

## On-screen (visual) hooks

- ≤8 words if static; or type-on word-by-word builds across 2-4 cuts (text motion + cut motion in the first 2s - nothing static in the opening).
- The on-screen line may DIFFER from the spoken line: spoken carries the sentence, on-screen carries the sharpest fragment ("BUT I CAN'T" on a black card).
- Fat condensed all-caps in an accent colour for headline beats; thin white lowercase for subtitles - two visibly different type systems (see visual-craft.md).
- First frame must work with sound OFF: the text hook + an interesting image = the real thumbnail.

## Rules

- **Hard ceiling: 14 words per spoken hook.** Count them. Over 14 and the gap closes before the scroll stops.
- **No antithesis or fragment triads in a hook**, ever. A hook built on "it's not X, it's Y" is a poster line, not a gap.
- Mechanic 2 (paradox opening an F9 inventory) is the default on tested data. Mechanic 12 is the strongest secondary. If neither is among the options offered, there should be a reason.
- **Say which lever each hook pulls.** Reach mechanics (8, 11, 5) and conversion mechanics (2 + F9) are not interchangeable, and the log shows the highest-reach hook converting worst.
- Always deliver 3-5 options spanning DIFFERENT mechanics, each labelled with mechanic + one line on the expected reaction.
- Numbers and named specifics outperform vague claims ("two businesses and a 9-5" > "so busy").
- First 3 words carry the scroll-stop; front-load the tension ("I lied", "I accidentally", "I can't").
- Never clickbait she can't cash: the video must fully pay the hook or trust burns.
- If every hook drafted is the same mechanic wearing different words, restart.
