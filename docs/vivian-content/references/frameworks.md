# Frameworks - extracted from the viral corpus (Batch 0: 11 reels)

Batch 0 (the original 11) produced F1-F7 and the ending styles. Batch 1 (11 more, Aug 2026, raw entries + autopsies in corpus.md) was classified against them and added F8, two new ending styles, three Format-radar shells and several hooks.

These are mechanisms, not templates. Combine, mutate, and add new ones via Mode 4. Every framework exists because it converts viewers into followers by revealing the creator.

## Why these work (the meta-pattern)

Across the whole corpus: (1) hook opens a gap in the first line, usually self-implicating; (2) the creator PROVES the surface story before subverting it, so the turn has weight; (3) one clearly-marked TURN inverts the framing; (4) the thesis is about the creator's identity, not general advice; (5) the ending withholds the hard CTA - self-roast, quiet line, loop-close, or series reveal. Follower growth correlates with unresolved identity ("what happens to HER next"), not with usefulness.

## F1 - The Confession Arc
*Source: "niche I can't monetize" (+1000 followers), "I lied to you guys" (STEAM not STEM)*

Beats: confession hook (self-implicating, present-tense problem) → credibility proof (show the success/traction is REAL, 5-10s) → the turn (constraint or hidden truth revealed, hard visual stop) → thesis (the transferable realisation, one sentence) → self-aware ending (self-roast or admission, NO CTA).

Why it converts: confessing a mistake from a position of visible success = intrigue + trust. The viewer follows to see how she resolves it.
Vivian fits: "I built a business I'm embarrassed to promote", "I make [$X] and I still feel behind", "my 9-5 is funding the thing that will replace it and my boss doesn't know the timeline."

Batch 1 update: "this is a me problem" (@liahansenn) is an F1 with a **self-diagnosis ending** - she names her own flaw as the punchline and leaves it unresolved ("I quit the 9-5, got the freedom, still not content... that's a me problem"). One of the strongest Vivian shapes in the batch (9-5 vs freelance is her exact lane). Guard from the same batch: @liahansenn's imposter-syndrome reel is an F1 that RESOLVES inside the video (she looks back and feels proud) and it won comments, not follows. F1 converts by withholding the resolution - keep the thread open.

## F2 - The Mirror
*Source: "I want to do everything, so I do nothing"*

Beats: paradox hook → second-person symptom read ("I know you. There are 16 tabs open in your browser right now" - hyper-specific, verifiable details of the viewer's life) → the turn: narrator flips the accusation onto herself with her own receipts → thesis about the shared condition → end mid-thought or hard cut (loops well).

Why it converts: being SEEN is the strongest share trigger; the self-implication converts it from lecture to kinship.
**Tested caveat (performance-log E3/E6):** F2 is a REACH framework. The tall poppy essay is pure F2 and took the biggest reach in the log (75k) at the worst conversion (1.33/1k), because the self-implication half was skipped. If the narrator's own receipts are not on screen, F2 is an accusation and converts at baseline. When in doubt, write it as F9 instead - inventory converts 3.75x better than accusation.
Vivian fits: analysis paralysis, "everyone can do it they're just lazy" (read the viewer their excuses, then admit hers), detachment, comparison.
Craft note: symptom lists need literal-proof b-roll (the actual tabs, the actual unread book).

## F3 - The Borrowed Concept
*Source: Japanese "three faces", Vietnamese "we don't say I love you"*

Beats: cold-open aphorism/linguistic fact over black → enumerate the concept with matched b-roll → pivot question to viewer OR pivot into first person → apply to her own life (this is the follow trigger - skip it and it's a Wikipedia post) → soft sincere payoff (the reveal that the video was really about love/identity), emoticon or quiet line, no CTA.

Why it converts: borrowed wisdom gives instant depth and save-ability; the personal application makes it hers.
Vivian fits (SPIN, never copy the "in X we don't say" wording again): a Chinese word with no English translation that explains her family; what her mum says instead of I love you; red string theory but applied to her breakup/solo trip; Chinese vs Western definitions of hard work; a concept her grandparents live by.

## F4 - The Inversion Essay
*Source: "first in the family to move away" ("people aren't in the way of the big thing - they ARE the big thing"), "dream job and I hated it"*

Beats: universal callout or contrarian-flex hook → light activity spine (packing gifts, a routine - gives the essay a body) → success montage (prove the dream is real) → values inversion at ~60-70% mark, stated in one chiasmus-like line → resolution in imagery, ending on a quiet emotional or mortality line. No CTA.

Why it converts: the inversion line is the shareable quote; the emotional floor drives comments; ambition + love of family in one video is exactly Vivian's core.
Vivian fits: "I want to retire at 30" inverted into "the money was never the point, my family is the point"; the solo trip; going back to China.

## F5 - The Quest / Series Pilot
*Source: "delusional for 100 days", "STEAM not STEM ep 1", "vibe coding pt 2", life-as-a-techie ep 13, and her own "can I set up my business in one day"*

Beats: quest hook (question or dare: "what would happen if...", "can I ___ in one day") → self-intro + stakes ("hi, I'm Vivian, and if I don't ___ I'll lose my mind") → provocative diagnosis of why this matters → the mission stated → invitation ("follow along" as invitation, or series title card reveal in the last 3s).

Why it converts: THE strongest follow mechanism in the corpus - a series makes following the only way to get the payoff. Numbered eps create sunk-cost viewing.
Vivian fits: bycasper build series (one day → first sale → first 100 orders → first hire), "13 weeks to ___" (the 13-week year AS a public quest), solo trip series, "opening a home cafe for my friends", make-an-app, personal-website. Every side quest she mentioned belongs here.

### F5 tested learning (bycasper ep 1 A/B, her own data)
Two VOs tested on the same footage; the F5+F6 hybrid beat the pure confession-quest. Strong prior (n=1, comment-bait confounder acknowledged):
- **Question hook > invitation hook.** "is it possible to start a business from a cafe in one day?" beat "launch a product with me from scratch" - a testable question carries a stake; an invitation doesn't.
- **Promise utility on top of story:** "these are the exact steps i'd follow if i was starting with no experience" gives a selfish reason to finish.
- **Steps spine with personality asides.** "first / then / after that" markers = retention scaffolding; keep her confession beats and jokes INSIDE the spine ("honestly, i probably shouldn't be starting another business" as beat 2, the casper SOURCE joke, "since it's my brand and i can do whatever i want") - structure from the winner, soul from the runner-up.
- **Credibility clause by line 3** (the six-figure month as a subordinate clause).
- **Dual CTA:** follow for the series + comment-keyword for a guide ("comment launch") - the comment engine drives distribution; series follow drives retention.
Default bycasper episode formula: question hook → confession stakes beat → credibility clause → steps spine with asides → live payoff → dual CTA.

Batch 1 update (F5): "STEAM not STEM ep 6" (@gracenahx) proves a personal-identity series survives to ep 6, and it runs ~2 min - well over the 30-60s default - and still holds. Long-form is a **series-essay exception**, not a new default: it works because a self-questioning hook plus the sunk cost of a numbered series carry the length. The Gazi "day in the life of a big tech engineer" DITL is the life-as-a-techie lane (stands in for the lost ep 13) - proof a raw, anti-aesthetic DITL can run as an F5-adjacent persona series. This is the model for Vivian's "show more of my actual life" goal: real footage earns follows via honesty + jokes, not cinematography.

## F6 - The Evidence Tutorial
*Source: vibe coding pt 2 (GitHub/domain/Vercel); "5 figures on Etsy with no social media" (@bysally.co) - Batch 1*

Beats: gap hook ("everyone talks about X, no one shows you how") → numbered steps (3 max), each with one visual anchor on screen → analogy for the hard concept → live payoff (the thing working) → the ONLY framework where a hard CTA is allowed ("save this and follow for more"), delivered as a gesture not an end card.

Why it converts: save-driven distribution; positions her as capable, which fuels the aspirational pillars.
Vivian fits: how to start a business (registration, first product, first sale), how she runs 13-week blocks, how she meal preps for the dog, business-in-a-day breakdowns.
Guard: max ~1 in 4 posts - tutorials get saves and views, identity gets follows.
Batch 1 update: the Etsy reel adds a **credibility-clause hook** ("I made five figures with zero social media") and a **"part two" cliffhanger** as a lightweight series hook bolted onto a tutorial - both directly usable for bycasper build/how-to posts. Strategic note: a tutorial in a high-paying niche (business, AI, productivity) also advertises that niche to brands - this is the F6 lever for Vivian's brand-deal goal (see pillars.md, "niche I can't monetize" lesson: what you post shapes the deals that come to you).

## F7 - The Silent Loop
*Source: Starbucks study timelapse*

No VO, no hook line, no CTA. Locked-off shot, title caption in first frame, a wordless progress-proof payoff (the drink drains = time passed), last frame matches first for seamless replay. 7-15s.
Why it converts: replay-count farming + aesthetic follow; low effort, high volume filler between essays.
Vivian fits: bycasper packing timelapse, harbour run sunrise, dog meal prep, work session. Use as connective tissue in the grid, 1-2 per week max.

## F8 - The Aphorism Montage
*Source: "what a privilege" (@michelleylens), "a girl full of contradictions" (@michelleylens) - Batch 1*

Beats: poetic anaphora VO (a repeated stem - "what a privilege it is to...", "a girl who...") over aesthetic b-roll → the list escalates → a single reframe or universal turn closes it. No confession/paradox hook, no receipts, no CTA. 20-45s.

Why it (barely) converts: this is the spoken-word cousin of F7 - it wins saves and aesthetic/vibe follows, not identity follows. The "being seen" version ("a girl full of contradictions") triggers comments and shares, but with no creator receipts it is F2 with the follow-half removed (see corpus autopsy - compare directly to "I want to do everything, so I do nothing").
Vivian fits: **only with a payload underneath.** The montage form is fine for Pillar 4 (heritage) or Pillar 5 texture IF each line carries one of HER specifics - a real object, a number, a family detail on screen. A pure aesthetic montage is the "some people follow me for the vibe" bucket she said she is OK with occasionally - cap it. Left unchecked it recruits followers who do not care about her life, which is her stated anti-goal.
Guard: F8 is filler-with-words, same tier as F7. Never let it crowd out the identity frameworks.

## F9 - The Self-Inventory (HIGHEST CONVERTING - tested)
*Source: her own "too many dreams" reel - 40k views, 200 follows, 5.00 follows/1k, 3.75x the next best video in performance-log.md (E6)*

Beats: paradox hook stating a feeling as a fact, no second person (<=10 words) -> the wants/list beat, enumerated fast and overlapping -> the admission of the strategy that produced the mess ("so instead of choosing, i started doing everything") -> **her own symptom inventory, forensically specific** (page 40, the unbuilt domain, the lesson booked yesterday) -> the cost line, delivered slow, stating what the mess costs her daily -> the received advice, quoted only to price it -> **self-accusation as an open question, aimed inward, unresolved.**

Why it converts: the viewer recognises themselves in her inventory without ever being addressed. Recognising yourself in someone's confession creates an obligation to that person; being told about yourself does not. This is the difference between F9 and F2, and it is worth 3.75x on follows-per-reach.

**The F2 contrast, which is the most important comparison in the whole log.** Dreams and the tall poppy essay close on the same rhetorical device - an accusing question about ambition. Tall poppy points it at the viewer ("if you felt bad, is it because you want the same thing?") and converts at 1.33/1k off the largest reach in the log. Dreams points it at herself ("i keep calling it ambition. but is it just commitment issues?") and converts at 5.00/1k. Same device, opposite target, 3.75x apart.

Craft rules that make it work (all three are load-bearing):
- **Specifics must be checkable and slightly embarrassing.** "Page 40", not "a book i didn't finish". "Three vibe coded apps i'm halfway through", not "some side projects". Vague inventory reads as a bit; precise inventory reads as a confession.
- **Flexes go inside the symptom list, never outside it.** "A beauty brand i want to grow to 7 figures this year" appears as a *problem*. The achievement becomes evidence for the confession and therefore cannot read as a flex. This is the cleanest execution of voice.md's understated-flex rule in the corpus.
- **No advice, at all.** The one piece of advice in Dreams is quoted in order to be priced. The moment F9 tells the viewer what to do it collapses into the shapes that convert at baseline.

Vivian fits: the contradictions idea (pillars.md seeded idea 7) is F9 and should be written as F9. Also: what she has actually abandoned this year, the gap between the businesses she runs and the ones she has only bought domains for, the investing "i know nothing" angle, wanting to be rich and wanting a soft life.

Guard: F9 requires real receipts she is willing to show as flaws. Without an inventory that costs her something it is F8 with a question mark on the end.

## Ending styles (pick deliberately, name it in output)

1. **Self-roast** - "and that's on being short-sighted." (F1)
2. **Quiet mortality/emotion line** - "until I can't anymore." (F4)
3. **Loop-close** - final frame/quote mirrors the open. (F2, F7)
4. **Series reveal** - title card in last 3s recontextualises the video as ep 1. (F5)
5. **Sincere sign-off** - emoticon-grade warmth, heritage content only. (F3)
6. **Soft ask** - save/follow via gesture, tutorials only. (F6)
7. **Self-diagnosis** - name your own flaw as the closing punchline and leave it unresolved. "this is a me problem." (F1, Batch 1) A sharper cousin of the self-roast; the unresolved flaw is the follow trigger.
8. **Reframe close** - a gratitude/values reframe as the last line, no CTA. "living the days we used to wish for." (F8) Save-y, follow-light.
9. **Inward self-accusation as an open question** - name the least flattering possible reading of your own behaviour, phrase it as a question, do not answer it. "i keep calling it ambition. but is it just commitment issues?" (F9, tested - the highest-converting ending in the log.) The rule is the direction: the same question aimed at the viewer converts at roughly a third of the rate (performance-log E6). Never answer it, and never soften it with a follow-up line.

Hard "follow me" CTAs anywhere else are banned - the corpus wins by withholding.
