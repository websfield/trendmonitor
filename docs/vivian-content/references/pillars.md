# Pillars + strategy

Positioning in one line: **a 23yo Chinese-Australian woman building her way out of a 9-5, saying the ambitious part out loud, without the grindset costume.**

The aspirational lane is crowded with cringe; her differentiation is bluntness + receipts + warmth for family. "Authentic aspirational" = she shows the numbers, admits the mess, and never performs motivation.

## Goals (stated by Vivian, Aug 2026) - read first

Two goals now sit above the pillar mix and should bias every call:

1. **Followers who actually care about her life** (not just the aesthetic). Practical implication: show MORE real life in the videos - convert footage to thesis (Mode 1), keep HER in frame, and use the anti-aesthetic DITL lever (visual-craft.md) + the expectation-subversion hook (hooks.md #13) so raw footage is allowed to look raw. The cinematic / aphorism-montage reels (F8, F7) are follow-light and recruit vibe-followers; she is OK with SOME of that occasionally, so keep them a minority (1-2/week max), never the default.
2. **Brand deals in high-paying industries** (business, AI/tech, productivity; content-creation-about-content later, after ~10k followers). This is a positioning lever, not a pitch. Per the "niche I can't monetize" corpus reel (F1), what you post shapes the deals that land in your inbox - so to attract high-CPM inbound, deliberately weight content toward business, building things with AI, and productivity, so the account reads as a business/tech/productivity creator. Keep the understated-flex and no-soul-selling rules (voice.md): she attracts the right deals by demonstrating the niche, she does not chase cringe ones.

These two goals pull against each other (real-life follows vs high-paying-niche positioning). Resolve it by making the business/AI/productivity content personal and stakes-driven - her actual businesses, her real numbers, her build-in-public - rather than generic how-to, so one post serves both.

## Pillar 1 - Rich Out Loud (money + ambition)
Retire at 30, salary/revenue transparency, what she spends in a week, financial freedom as a family project not a flex, 13-week year in public.
Primary frameworks: F1 Confession, F2 Mirror, F4 Inversion. Follow trigger: "will she actually do it" - an open, numbered ambition is a series in disguise.
Controversy budget lives here: "I want to be rich", "everyone can, most are lazy", "quit your job" takes. One spike post per 2 weeks max so it reads as conviction, not rage-bait.

## Pillar 2 - Building bycasper (+ business brain)
The bycasper series is the tentpole: numbered quest episodes (one day → first sale → first 100 → first stockist). Joie Beauty = the "I've done this before" credibility layer. Practical + emotional business advice (pick a business you're proud of; lifestyle-fit).
Primary frameworks: F5 Quest (tentpole), F6 Tutorial (≤1 in 4), F1 Confession (when things go wrong - failures are the best episodes).
Rule: document, don't teach, by default. Teaching posts borrow authority; documenting posts build it.
AI / build-in-public / productivity (Aug 2026): "building random things with AI", automating her life/business with Claude skills, and productivity systems belong here - they are high-paying-niche signals (Goal 2) as much as content. Document her actually building/using the tools (the Week13 app, her Claude automations), never generic productivity advice. Content-creation-about-content is a LATER pillar - hold it until ~10k followers, then it becomes a credibility/monetisation layer.

## Pillar 3 - The Essays (perspective / substack-to-reel)
Detachment, control, red string theory, audacity, imposter syndrome in Australia (tall poppy syndrome - underused angle), Chinese vs Western work culture, dating discourse through her own lens only, "you want to do everything so you do nothing"-class ideas.
Primary frameworks: F2 Mirror, F3 Borrowed Concept, F4 Inversion. Source pipeline: substack articles and YouTube video essays she finds - always rebuilt through her stakes (Mode 3), never summarised.

## Pillar 4 - Blood + Country (heritage + family)
Feeling Chinese in China without growing up there, wanting her kids to speak Chinese, what her parents did instead of saying I love you, the brother, giving her family a nice life (this pillar is WHY pillar 1 exists - link them often), travel with meaning ("a country I never grew up in" > "day in Shanghai").
Primary frameworks: F3 Borrowed Concept, F4 Inversion. The only pillar where fully earnest is correct. Highest share + save potential; sentimental formats spread through tags ("sending this to my mum").

## Pillar 5 - The Life (converted DITL / fitness / dog / spending)
Harbour runs, pilates, the nice gym, the dog (meal prep content = niche gold), friends, travel days, what I eat/spend. NEVER posted as-is - always converted via Mode 1 into a thesis owned by pillars 1-4, or posted as F7 Silent Loops for grid texture.
This pillar is evidence, not subject.

## Mix (weekly rhythm at ~4-5 posts)
- 1 tentpole (F5 series ep or a big F1/F4 essay)
- 1-2 essays (pillar 3 or 4)
- 1 converted-life or money post
- 1 F7 silent loop as filler
Trends: occasional, only when a trend can carry one of her theses. Skip otherwise.

## North-star metrics
Follows per reach (not views), comment depth (stories shared back, not emoji), saves on tutorials/heritage, series episode-2 retention. A video with 20k views and 5 follows is a worse video than 4k views and 80 follows - judge every autopsy against this.
