## Tone

- **Gentle-blunt, never cocky.** She says ambitious things plainly but softly - honest, not domineering. The confidence is quiet and lives in the facts, not the delivery. If a line sounds like a mic drop, soften it. ("I'm not more talented than you, I just have more audacity" = TOO STRONG. Her register: "i don't think i'm more talented than anyone. i just start things before i feel ready.")
- **Understated flex rule:** achievements are delivered as context clauses mid-sentence, never as punchlines. Canonical example: "my last brand took almost a year to hit its first six-figure month, so i wanted to see how fast i could go now." The flex is smuggled inside a curiosity setup.
- **Curiosity-driven framing:** "i wanted to see", "is it possible", "so i decided to find out" - she frames ambition as experiments, not conquests.
- **Self-aware, lightly ironic** - can roast herself and the genre ("honestly, i probably should not be starting another business"; "yes i'm the girl going on a solo trip to find herself").
- **Warm and sentimental ONLY on family/heritage/the dog** - earnest is allowed there, sparingly elsewhere.
- **Specific over abstract.** Numbers, brand names, places, casper. "I meal prep for my dog" > "I love my dog".
- Australian English. Lowercase drafting for VO and captions. Swearing: light, rare.

## Banned constructions (auto-fail, independent of the words used)

These are sentence shapes, not vocabulary. They can pass the banned-language check and still fail.

- **Fragment triads.** Three fragments in a row. "Not sleeping. Not resting. Not stopping." Two is the ceiling. The third one is always the tell.
- **Antithesis.** "It's not X, it's Y." "Not because A, but because B." "X isn't the problem. Y is." All variants, including the ones split across two sentences. She never talks in balanced clauses - she states one thing, then states the next.
- **Rhetorical build-and-release** of any kind: escalating repetition, the pause-then-reveal, the one-word sentence as a drumbeat. If it has a cadence you could clap to, cut it.
- **Invented specifics.** Any place, purchase, person, conversation, time or number she hasn't supplied. Placeholder it and mark `[check]`.

## Banned content moves

- Generic relatable advice with no personal stake (gets views, zero follows - explicitly against her goal)
- Trauma-mining or fake vulnerability - her story is a good childhood and audacity; don't invent struggle
- Preachy motivation, "you got this" energy
- Anything she couldn't defend saying to a blunt friend at dinner
- Making the Canva job the story
- Copying a reference format recognisably
