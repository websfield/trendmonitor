## Decisions baked in

- **Voice-build robustness without a second vendor call:** `AssemblyError` gains a closed `kind` (16 sites); the evidence-quote match stays **exact first** and, only on a miss, tolerates the characters of one explicit code-point table (typographic quotes and dashes, whitespace runs, a paragraph token) behind a runtime postcondition that fails a mis-map closed as `quote_not_found`; it **stores the creator's original slice** as the quote, because the database's own provenance check refuses anything else (`with-workspace.ts:5737`); the refused state carries the kind and a static sentence per kind (owner decision 2026-09-15, quote-matcher redesign). - **Refusal copy references only its own state (standing rule, 2026-09-15; scoped 2026-09-15 after batch 8):** a refusal sentence may reference only the state the refusal itself carries, **or a stable *location* where the authoritative record lives — never a *value* rendered elsewhere** (a price, a balance, another line's wording), and never a policy. Where a refusal must point at a line on the same screen, that line is **pinned outside every fold and asserted** (T7's `studio-mode-note`) rather than described. Three of the five fix-induced defects in the batch-5 round were the same authoring move — replacing a false claim with a new claim about something else on the screen — and this rule is what makes the correct move a *subtraction*. **Why "another page" was struck:** as first written the rule forbade "the content of another line, another page or a policy", which forbade the very sentence adopted to satisfy it ("…is in your credit history on the usage page"), and two more live sentences in this plan's own scope — T8's tier block and `studio-no-modes` (`studio-panel.tsx:233-238`). Found by billing and compliance independently in batch 7 and again in batch 8, and not cosmetic: as written the rule instructs the implementer to delete the pointer, and a refusal with no location is a refusal with no way forward (DESIGN.md "refusals name the remedy"; lesson 2026-07-30). A durable location stays legal; the superseded "…and your balance" wording stays forbidden, which is the distinction that matters.
