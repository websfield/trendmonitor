## Objective

Four ways this product can take money and deliver nothing, plus the silent-loss items that share their shape. The payments room's own summary is "the ledger core is the strongest code in this repo and I could not break it — every finding is at the edges", and that is exactly what this phase touches: the edges, not the fold.

The register's CRITICAL here is a promise with no producer: `generate.ts:577-582` says a retry "settles now… and the candidate survives the wait", nothing settles a stranded `vendor_complete` attempt, and the only alert fires **at the instant the paid evidence is destroyed**. The cheap half of that — alerting before the 24-hour clear instead of after it — removes the loss of *visibility* immediately; the expensive half (automatic settlement) is a decision with a trigger, because `settle` is private and a general producer is a larger change than the register implies.

**Lesson that bites here,** verbatim: *2026-09-09 — **Isolating a failure so the batch survives it converts a LOUD failure into a SILENT one**: the try/catch ships in the same change as a count that reaches an alert, or a poisoned item retries forever unseen — and the comment saying the count is reported is not the reporting.* Three of this phase's requirements are that lesson's exact shape.

**Second lesson,** because the plan gate found it bites the input ceiling: *2026-07-30 — Fail closed, but never without a way forward.* A ceiling on the assembled prompt refuses a creator whose brain has grown, and a refusal that says only "too large" is the outage. P3-R2 names the way forward.

## Reachability

Both config keys are reachable from `/admin/config` once `config:migrate` materialises them (`.default()` keys are carried by `mergeMissing`, `schema.ts:412-416`).

## Least confident

The input-token number. It is a margin dial with no measured provenance anywhere in the repo — the two existing constants (100,000 and 40,000) were chosen, not derived — and the 40,000 default here is the Sample Spin draft ceiling reused because it is the one number a real tenant-shaped prompt has been sent under. The card records the largest legitimate assembled prompt it measured (the brain-heavy fixture, not only the input-heavy one) and the chosen ceiling's headroom over it, so the number is a recorded choice rather than a guess presented as a bound; the key is config precisely so the first measured month can move it without a deploy.
