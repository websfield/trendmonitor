## Objective

The docs room's summary: "Product screens unusually careful; the marketing surface sells three things that do not exist." The landing page sells a learning loop that cannot run (R-115 admits only `connector_verified` results and the product's own copy says no connector exists), a weekly digest whose loader `throw`s, and a trends feed whose only producer is a blocker. The depth read found five more on the same page, including two sentences that are false in both halves.

The fix is copy plus **one instrument**, and the objective is stated as what the instrument proves (merged L-5): every `PRICING[].lines[]` entry resolves to one of three pin kinds evaluated against the tree, every rendered marketing surface is scanned against the claims canon with a planted specimen per label, and three prose sentences that describe a bound are pinned to the constant that bounds them. What the instrument does **not** prove, and this phase does not claim: that the marketing-claims *class* is closed. R-132 §1 records the measurement — **17 of 17** plausible sales sentences pass the canon, and `MARKETING_CLAIM_GAPS` holds the six escaping shapes as an asserted absence on every rendered surface. Closing the class needs a generator over {subject} × {outcome} × {hedge}, the audit's own round-3 conclusion for `claims.ts`; it is a deferral-ledger row in the master plan with a trigger, not a task here.

`learn` is on the forbidden list. `landing-sections.tsx:135` read *"Log results. It learns you."* (pre-rewrite; removed under R-132 — `grep "It learns you\|learns your voice" app/(marketing)/` returns nothing on 2026-09-21) — the word was in the canon, the canon had 16 working consumers, and **not one of them rendered this page**.

**Lesson that bites here,** verbatim: *2026-08-26 — a verifier that reports success is not verified until something OUTSIDE it tries to break it… every scanner must assert it catches a PLANTED violation of each shape it claims to cover.*
