## Objective

REQ-A04 is a rights obligation, and today a solo creator's right to erasure has no terminating path. Three defects interlock:

1. `assertNotLastOwner` refuses whenever the person is sole active owner of *any* workspace and never looks at whether that workspace is already being deleted; the printed remedy names two surfaces — ownership transfer and owner invitation — that the code's own comment says **do not exist**; and deleting the workspace mints a replacement the person is again sole owner of, on the next render.
2. The state machine's `blocked` state has no exit to `cancelled` while all three of its pre-erasure predecessors do, and a crash between the journal reservation and the tombstone transaction wedges an operation in `requested` permanently — with **five documented resume seams that have zero production callers and are exported from nothing**.
3. The replacement workspace hides the pending deletion from the only surface that can cancel it, and cancelling then throws "user belongs to multiple workspaces" on every product page.

All of this is latent today — `RESPIN_DELETION_REQUEST_SCOPES` is unset, so no creator can request a deletion. That is what makes this the right moment to fix it: the state machine can be corrected while nothing is in flight.

Phase 5 also closes the one item that makes a paying customer unreachable: a Google-only creator cannot subscribe, buy credits, pause, resume or open the cancellation portal, because every billing action inner-joins `providerId = "credential"` and **no enrolment surface exists anywhere**.

**Lessons that bite here:** 2026-07-30 (*fail closed, but never without a way forward* — read the refusal's own printed remedy); 2026-08-21 (runtime, not only typed).

## Reachability

A sole-owner creator can request their own erasure on `/settings/account`, see it reach a terminal state, and cancel a pending workspace deletion from the same page (callers: `app/(product)/settings/account/actions.ts` and `page.tsx`, rows 7–8, this phase, holding the read-grade scope from row 13 → row 9); during the grace window they can still export their brain through `GET /api/export` (row 12) and read its history on `/brain` (row 16). **Those callers are live only for a deployment whose `RESPIN_DELETION_REQUEST_SCOPES` opens the scope** — a runtime toggle, not a deferred phase; with the flag unset (today's state everywhere) the request controls render closed (`account-view.tsx:45`) and nothing in this phase is reachable by a creator except the Google sign-in gate. A new visitor no longer sees a Google sign-in the product cannot bill (`app/(auth)/auth-form.tsx`, row 27, on `/sign-in` and `/sign-up`).

## Least confident

Whether `blocked → cancelled` is the right edge or whether `blocked` should instead be unreachable pre-grace. The register says the asymmetry is unexplained, which means the decision was never made rather than made and recorded. R-139 makes it. The batch-0 gate sharpened the risk: it is three guard sites, not one, and a fix at one of them goes green on the pair table while the real path still refuses — which is why AC5 drives the real path on live Postgres. The phase's honest fallback — if the edge turns out to be wrong — is the operator surface that `blocked_awaiting_operator` already assumes exists, and that surface has no rows here: taking the fallback means a new phase, not a quiet substitution.
