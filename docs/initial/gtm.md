# Go-to-Market: Respin

**Companion to:** `PRD.md`. One page on purpose. The economics dictate the strategy: at a $10 entry tier with normal consumer churn, lifetime gross profit per Creator-tier user is roughly $80-120 while average subscription CAC runs ~$72 - so paid acquisition is off until LTV is proven, and every channel below is organic or product-led.

## Positioning

**Primary claim:** Scripts in your voice, built on mechanisms proven to perform, that get better with every post you log.
**The one-screen proof:** the same idea generated generic vs through a creator brain, side by side. This comparison is the answer to "why not just ChatGPT" and it leads the landing page, the demo, and every piece of founder content.
**Never claimed:** virality, guaranteed reach, follower counts (REQ-I04 is a marketing rule too).
**Against incumbents (Spotter $49, vidIQ $39, 1of10 $29, OutlierKit $29-49):** they stop at ideas, titles, and thumbnails; Respin outputs the filmable script in your voice and holds your evidence. Comparison pages for each are cheap, high-intent SEO - the incumbents publish them against each other constantly, which marks the search demand.

## The wedge

Launch persona: **YouTube Shorts creators** (decision R-11). Trend data is real there on day one, incumbents have educated the market on paying for ideation, and the script+brain output wins the head-to-head. TikTok/IG-native creators are the expansion, entered via licensed data once pilots name the demand. Messaging still speaks "short-form creator" - the wedge is targeting, not identity.

## Channels, ranked by expected contribution

1. **Founder-creator flywheel.** Vivian builds and uses the product in public: the founding case study (her best-converting post came from this method), weekly build-and-results content, her scripts visibly produced through her own brain. The product markets itself by being its own evidence. Lowest cost, highest trust, starts now - during the pilot, not after.
2. **Evidence-led SEO.** Framework-of-the-week (REQ-H03), public autopsies of outperforming Shorts, and vs-competitor pages. Everything published follows the product's own voice rules - the marketing must not read as AI slop when the product's pitch is not sounding like AI slop.
3. **Creator-educator affiliates.** The people selling "grow on YouTube" courses want tools to recommend. 30% recurring commission for 12 months via Rewardful or Tolt (decision R-13). Recruit from the pilot's orbit; launch post-M6.
4. **In-product referral.** Give 200 credits, get 200 credits on the referred user's first paid month. Credits, not cash - it feeds usage, which feeds retention.
5. **Community.** A small Discord for pilot creators first; scale only if it earns it. Not a launch dependency.

## Launch sequence

1. **Pilot (during/after M6):** 5-10 hand-recruited Shorts creators across niches. Their logged results are the launch asset; the build-plan's evidence criterion is also the marketing gate - no public "it works" claim until it reads green.
2. **Waitlist + build-in-public:** open during pilot; convert with founding-member pricing (lock $10/$60 for a year).
3. **Public launch:** case studies with real numbers and named confounders (honesty as differentiation), comparison demo front and centre, affiliates activated.

## Retention as GTM

44% of subscription cancellations land in the first 90 days and half of AI subscribers cancel-and-restart by habit. Counters, all in product: first-session aha (REQ-B04), weekly digest as the habit hook, the brain as a visibly accumulating asset (usage page shows brain version count and tested rules - what they'd walk away from), pause instead of cancel (REQ-G08), and win-back email with the brain intact.

## Metrics that decide things

Signups, activation, and free→paid **by source**; affiliate share of new MRR; referral participation; blended CAC (target < $25 at Creator tier before any paid spend is even discussed); pilot evidence criterion status. Reviewed against the margin dashboard weekly, same cadence, same honesty rules.
