# Go-to-Market: Respin

**Companion to:** `PRD.md`. One page on purpose. Paid acquisition is off until production cohorts establish retention, recognised revenue, acquisition cost, and complete model-spend cost. The launch channels below are organic or product-led; no LTV, CAC, churn, or margin estimate is an outward claim.

## Positioning

**Primary launch claim:** Scripts in your voice, built on reviewed mechanisms, with every durable brain change shown for approval. Self-reported results remain visible but do not drive numerical learning; result-based learning stays unavailable until a real connector supplies verified evidence (R-115).
**The one-screen proof:** one real, clearly labelled Sample Spin through a fictional brain and synthetic reference, with the original beside only the gate-passed transformation and the shaping rules highlighted. It is transformation proof, not performance proof; no generic no-brain control is generated (R-116).
**Never claimed:** virality, guaranteed reach, follower counts (REQ-I04 is a marketing rule too).
**Against incumbents:** comparison pages are withheld until a dated claim ledger cites each competitor's current official pricing and capabilities and an outbound-truth review passes. Respin may describe only its own shipped behavior: producing a filmable script through a creator-controlled brain with explicit evidence and approval boundaries.

## The wedge

Launch persona: **YouTube Shorts creators** (decision R-11). The YouTube Data API is the first compliant adapter; live day-one availability remains evidence-gated and is not inherited from the current `ALMOST` trend slice. Incumbents have educated the market on paying for ideation. TikTok/IG-native creators are the expansion, entered via licensed data once pilots name the demand. Messaging still speaks "short-form creator" - the wedge is targeting, not identity.

## Channels, ranked by expected contribution

1. **Founder-creator build-in-public.** Vivian may show workflow, product decisions, and scripts produced through her own brain. Her prior experience is an anecdotal founding input, not a causal case study or evidence that Respin improves performance. Results content waits for connector-verified treatment and baseline evidence with n and confounders.
2. **Evidence-led SEO.** Framework-of-the-week (REQ-H03) may publish mechanism-only, rights-cleared material. Public autopsies require a compliant source, attribution, and an approved public-rights path; “outperforming” and competitor comparisons wait for the dated claim ledger and outbound review. Everything published follows the product's own voice rules.
3. **Creator-educator affiliates.** The people selling "grow on YouTube" courses want tools to recommend. 30% recurring commission for 12 months via Rewardful or Tolt (decision R-13). Recruit from the pilot's orbit; launch post-M6.
4. **In-product referral.** Deferred concept only. No credit amount or referral promise is published until a separately approved fulfillment, fraud, expiry, accounting, and lifecycle design ships.
5. **Community.** A small Discord for pilot creators first; scale only if it earns it. Not a launch dependency.

## Launch sequence

1. **Pilot (during/after M6):** 5-10 hand-recruited Shorts creators across niches. Connector-verified treatment and personal-baseline results are the evidence gate; until a post-pilot connector exists the metric is unavailable and no public “it works” claim can pass.
2. **Waitlist + build-in-public:** open during pilot. Any founding-member price or price-lock promise must come from versioned product configuration plus implemented fulfillment terms; none is authorised by this document.
3. **Public launch:** case studies with real verified numbers and named confounders only after the evidence gate passes; the gated Sample Spin is front and centre; affiliates activate without efficacy guarantees.

## Retention as GTM

Retention work is measured from Respin cohorts rather than borrowed market statistics. Product levers are first-session aha (REQ-B04), the weekly digest as a candidate habit hook, the brain as a visibly accumulating asset, pause instead of cancel (REQ-G08), and a permissioned win-back path. None is called effective before evidence exists.

## Metrics that decide things

Signups, mature-cohort activation, and free→paid **by source**; affiliate share of new MRR; referral participation only after referral fulfillment exists; measured blended CAC; pilot evidence criterion status. Reviewed weekly against recognised revenue and complete model costs; every margin number is withheld for an incomplete grain (R-120), and no target is presented as achieved evidence.
