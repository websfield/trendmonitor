## Joie Beauty — imagined participant answers

**Public basis.** The shop offers lip glaze, blush balm and vanity cases, with season-based shade navigation. Its FAQ directs shoppers to a Season Finder quiz. Its February 2025 brand article connects the business to `@joiebeautyco` and advocates selecting fewer suitable products. Those are website claims and positioning, not observed Instagram behaviour. [Shop](https://joiebeauty.co/), [FAQ](https://joiebeauty.co/pages/faqs), [brand article](https://joiebeauty.co/blogs/learn-more/changing-the-beauty-industry).

- **What I want — imagined:** “Help me make choosing a shade feel manageable. Give me useful, attractive videos that explain the choice before asking someone to buy.”
- **Voice — imagined:** “Warm, considered and specific. I would rather show one application clearly than sound like every shade is essential.”
- **Audience hypothesis:** People uncertain about shade selection; actual follower demographics are unknown.
- **Production default:** One adult presenter, phone, available products, consistent light; 45 minutes for a batch of two 20–35-second Instagram Reels. Hands-only alternative if face presentation is uncomfortable.
- **Desired output:** Three distinct concepts, one spoken script, four to six shots, exact product/shade placeholders and one quiz CTA. Keep the filming plan within the declared kit.
- **Would reject — imagined:** “Don't invent a wear test, skin benefit, testimonial or claim that one shade works for everyone. Don't script a personal founder story I never gave you.”
- **Proposed signal:** Relevant shade-quiz visits; purchases tracked separately if attributable. No target rate or existing baseline is known.

## Cognito Tuition — imagined participant answers

**Public basis.** Cognito offers Sydney Year 11–12 HSC STEM tutoring. Its schedule distinguishes school-specific teaching order, standard/accelerated/holiday options; the FAQ describes original resources and student self-review with marking criteria. Its two-lesson trial applies to weekly courses and is charged if the student continues. [Schedule](https://www.cognitotuition.com/schedule), [FAQ](https://www.cognitotuition.com/faq), [official site](https://www.cognitotuition.com/).

- **What I want — imagined:** “Help a student understand one thing they were stuck on, and show a parent what our teaching feels like. Give our tutors something useful they can film between classes.”
- **Voice — imagined:** “Patient, precise and encouraging. Explain the missing reasoning step without making a student feel foolish.”
- **Audience hypothesis:** HSC STEM students and parents assessing teaching fit; keep the student lesson and parent decision message in separate videos.
- **Production default:** One adult tutor, phone and whiteboard/tablet; 45-minute batch, 30–60-second Reels. No students on camera. Tutor checks every solution and selects the applicable course/year.
- **Desired output:** A checked problem statement, concise worked explanation, readable on-screen notation and optional trial CTA. Teaching clarity takes priority over an aggressive hook.
- **Would reject — imagined:** “Don't promise a mark increase, borrow a student's story or compress the explanation until it is wrong. Don't call our trial unconditionally free.”
- **Proposed signal:** Relevant weekly-course trial enquiries; saves as a separate learning-interest signal, never proof of enrolment.

## How to use the drafts

Work through one brief per account first; keep edits against the explicitly assumed time, kit and voice. Then try the second brief to see whether the workflow supports a different topic/form. Record simulated objections as design feedback only. Leave actual participant acceptances, independent ratings and outcome fields empty until those events happen. These ten examples have been exposed to the development team and can never become unseen launch evidence.
