## What you are being asked to do

We are evaluating how well Respin turns a creator's approved context and practical constraints into concepts, scripts and filming plans. We compare two versions of the workflow. This evaluates preparation quality; it does not establish future views, sales, earnings or business results.

You will supply up to four briefs under the proposed allocation and review the resulting concepts and selected scripts. You choose whether any concept is worth developing. You and a named independent rater separately judge whether a selected script can be filmed with your stated resources. You may say that none is suitable. You do not have to publish, film, buy a subscription or give us access to your social-media account.

Participation is voluntary. You may decline a requested use below, skip a task or stop. All four uses are necessary for this particular evaluation, so declining one means we cannot include your briefs in it. It does not require you to accept marketing, public use of your content or unrelated research.

## Information and processing

The study records only the context and samples you specifically approve, your briefs and filming constraints, generated concepts/scripts/shot plans, your selections and ratings, the independent ratings, and the operation records needed to interpret the evaluation. The research owner records which materials you approved before collecting them. Do not supply passwords, account tokens, confidential client records or other people's personal information. Third-party appearances, student material and customer testimonials are outside this permission unless separately authorised and specifically listed.

Your approved context and briefs will be entered into separate research-owned Respin profiles for the two workflows. They will be processed by the disclosed model provider. The implementation currently uses Anthropic's API; the research owner must confirm the actual account, hosting locations and provider terms before the session. Processing may occur outside Australia. Do not describe the service as Australian-only or zero-retention unless that arrangement has been verified and disclosed.

Anthropic's published API policy generally deletes inputs and outputs within 30 days, with exceptions including different agreed terms, policy enforcement and legal obligations. That provider retention is separate from the research team's deletion schedule. The study will not opt your material into model training or submit it as provider feedback. These are conditions for operating this study, not a claim that a particular provider-account setting has already been checked. [Anthropic API retention policy](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996866-how-long-do-you-store-my-organization-s-data).

## Who may see the material

Only the named research owner, research operator, independent rater(s) and evaluation deployment/database administrator may access the study content required for their role. You may see your own briefs and outputs, not another participant's. The research owner must verify this access list for the storage, evaluation applications, databases, logs and backups before collection.

Fred, as product/launch owner, and anyone implementing, directing or tuning L1–L3 will not receive held-out briefs, outputs, individual ratings, reasons, errors or the arm key, either during or after the evaluation. After the look closes, they may receive only the summaries permitted by the evaluation method, without per-brief records or quoted reasons. A person's name appearing on a proposed team list is not evidence that they have accepted the role.

Research reports use participant codes rather than names or handles. A small coded study may still be recognisable to someone who knows the participants; we do not promise complete anonymity. Your identity, face, voice, quotes and content will not be published, used in advertising or put into a shared creator library under this permission. Any such use requires a separate, specific agreement.

## Retention and withdrawal

**Proposed study schedule:** delete identifiable research copies, the identity key and signed study records within 90 days after the evaluation closes or is abandoned. Retained backups must expire within 30 further days. The research owner must accept and verify that this schedule can be carried out before offering this form; replace it in the private copy if a different schedule is agreed, and tell the participant before they consent. Retention required by a provider or law must be disclosed separately, not silently folded into this promise.

You can withdraw by contacting the named research support channel above. The research owner will stop further use or processing under your consent when the request is received and arrange deletion of the team's identifiable working copies within 30 days, with backup expiry as disclosed above. Processing already completed cannot be undone, and copies retained by a provider follow its disclosed terms. Ask the research owner for the status of any deletion request.

If you withdraw after the brief set has been frozen, the study may retain a minimal coded record that a brief was withdrawn and include that withdrawal in its denominator. It will not retain your withdrawn content or reasons in reports. Withdrawal is not a judgement about you or your content. Summaries already distributed cannot necessarily be recalled; any later permitted reporting records the withdrawal without reusing your material.
