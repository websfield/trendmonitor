MOTION

120ms hovers, 200ms state changes, 360ms result entrances, one ease: cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1). Motion only states something: a run in flight pulses, a result rises once, a hover lifts. Nothing loops decoratively, and everything stops under prefers-reduced-motion.

VOICE · COPY RULES

Sentence case everywhere. Mono uppercase is for labels and meta only. No em-dashes in UI copy. Colons, periods and commas do the work. Numbers are mono and exact: 2.3x, 3.2 per 1k, never rounded theater. No promises of reach. The weakest point of every concept is disclosed. Refusals name a way forward. Empty states say why and what fills them. Unknown numbers say unavailable and why. A fake zero is a lie.
